# Civil service rules review - Haaziry

Review date: 30 October 2026. Source: uploaded Maldives Civil Service Regulation 2014, Regulation 2014/R-311, 220 PDF pages, effective 1 December 2014. Compared with the implementation at C:/Code projects/HAS-HR and the school's previously supplied leave table. Relevant pages reviewed in full: 5-6, 117-136 and 183-189; attendance and certificate pages also rendered for visual verification.

This is a gap analysis, not a finding that Haaziry implements every current civil-service requirement. The uploaded English document is the original 2014 version, not a verified current consolidation. Article 2 requires compliance with later amendments. CSC publishes 2025 amendments and a September 2026 circular concerning Articles 181 and 184. Do not substitute old numerical limits for later applicable entitlements without the relevant circulars.

## Period accounting follow-up implemented on 3 November 2026

Annual, Family and Sick now support service-anniversary periods, joining/approved cycle dates, explicit calendar-policy settings, per-period opening usage, shared sick resets and cross-period day allocation. See leave-policy.md. Earlier calendar-only gaps below describe the original review state. No dates are inferred and no prior opening amounts are automatically remapped; eligibility, service-credit decisions, expiry/carryover and some per-occasion validation remain outside this update.

## Authorized follow-up implemented on 30 October 2026

The user authorized the MC workflow and alignment of circumcision/Hajj/Umra to the supplied rules. Those prior gaps are now addressed by the MC receipt/verification/notification and HR-review workflow, five-day circumcision entitlement for either parent per occasion excluding holidays, and contextual pilgrimage eligibility/travel/balance calculations. See leave-policy.md for the active profile. Older records are flagged for review rather than rewritten. The comparison below records the initial findings; service-year cycles, full shift handling and other unselected provisions remain outstanding. The supplied 2014 English text is the explicitly selected rules basis for these categories, not a verified current consolidation of every amendment.

## Changes applied in the initial review

1. Sick without MC: retain the school's 15-day annual sublimit within the shared 30-day sick allowance; additionally limit a request to two consecutive counted working days. Validate adjacent pending/approved/completed requests for the same staff member so separate forms cannot bypass the limit. Configured holidays and Friday/Saturday are skipped consistently with the school's day-counting rules. Check at preview, create/update and approval. A working day without such leave breaks a sequence. Relevant source: Article 190(a),(f), PDF pages 134-135; 2020 official amendment announcement confirms the 15-day annual limit and two-day rule.
2. Attendance corrections: require a separately recorded evidence/verification reference, as well as the existing mandatory reason and authenticated Administrator account. Store it with the existing original-punch snapshots and before/after audit record. Display it in correction history. Relevant source: Article 172(b)-(d), PDF page 120. The field records the administrator's evidence reference; it does not independently verify the evidence or upload a document.
3. Preserve prior history and existing school limits. No automatic deduction, disciplinary sanction or rewriting of historical leave has been introduced.

## Inputs needed to complete alignment

- School/HR's applicable current amendments and circulars, especially circumcision, Hajj/Umra and maternity/paternity counting, plus the approved arrival schedule.
- Staff employment/service commencement dates and relevant prior-service history; confirmation of the applicable leave cycle and opening balances. Records crossing a cycle must be apportioned rather than all charged by start year.
- Shift end times/durations, notification procedure, certificate/form submission workflow, authorized reviewer roles and evidence storage location.
