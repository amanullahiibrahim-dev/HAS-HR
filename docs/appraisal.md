# Annual Appraisal

Appraisal provides Days Required to Attend, Days Attended, Days Attended (On Time), Absent Days, Disciplinary Actions, Appraisal Report and Supervisor. The selected year and employee/supervisor filters apply across these pages and their exports.

Appraisal attendance always uses the scheduled working calendar. Required = scheduled working days within the appraisal/employment period minus applicable approved/completed leave on those working days. Attended = required minus absent; on-time = attended minus late. Late attendance remains attendance, not absence. Friday/Saturday (or the supplied office weekly off days) and configured official closed dates are excluded before calculating all three measures. Joining date determines the employment start; the PIN-reuse/import cutoff only identifies missing evidence, not days outside employment. Past years cover January–December and the current year stops at the current Maldives date. Future dates are excluded. The report never uses a fixed annual target such as 216 or credits weekends as attended days.

The combined roster and employee View show required, attended, on-time and absent counts, with disciplinary actions separate. View includes scheduled days, applicable leave deductions, late counts/dates, absent dates and the on-time proportion. The percentage is informational, not a scored appraisal section. An example of 261 scheduled days minus 45 applicable leave days yields 216 required; with no absences and two late days, attended is 216 and on-time is 214 (99.07%). This is a regression fixture, not an assertion about actual school records.

Only approved/completed personal leave is deducted, limited to dates actually reached in the reporting period and the earlier of approved end/actual return. Pending/declined applications and official duty are not personal leave taken. Counting follows each saved leave type and basis, including holidays, Hajj travel extras and no-pay days. Per-date leave weights are capped at one, so overlaps are not deducted twice; half-day amounts are retained. Cross-year leave is allocated by dated usage. Manual usage that cannot be reconciled with the dated period is marked Leave review.

Attendance statuses come from the shared attendance-row builder, preserving corrections, role-based lateness, official duty and missing-history rules. Unclassified punches, outside-history dates and awaiting punches remain Unverified; attended/on-time totals are not finalized when these exist. No saved attendance, leave, entitlements, machine logs or previous reports are modified by appraisal calculations. Deleting or editing leave, recording corrections, or changing closed dates refreshes the report with the current source records.


## Supervision and disciplinary records

Appraisal navigation places Absent Days after Days Attended (On Time), then Disciplinary Actions and Appraisal Report, with Supervisor after the report. Each attendance subpage uses the same working calendar and leave deductions; the Absent Days page shows required days, confirmed absences, late days and unverified evidence separately. The combined report uses an employee roster with role, supervisor, supervision period, required/attended/on-time/absent days, recorded disciplinary-action count and a View control. Employment status and target columns are not included. Employee search and a searchable Supervisor button filter the report. CSV includes supervisor periods and disciplinary summaries.

The Supervisor page shows a single-choice supervisor radio list and a staff checkbox list immediately. Both lists can be filtered without clearing selections. Select matching staff adds the filtered group to the current selection, and Clear selection removes staff ticks. The Principal appears only under Minister of Education; school supervisors cannot supervise themselves.

Supervisor assignments have an inclusive effective-from and optional inclusive last-supervised date. Administrators can assign several school staff to one school supervisor, or edit an existing assignment with its current revision and a reason. Historical periods are retained; overlapping assignments, self-supervision and time-overlapping cycles are rejected. The Principal has Minister of Education as a fixed default supervisor; other school employees must be assigned a supervisor from the active school roster. A Principal may supervise other school staff. Year filters clip supervision to that year and the report's through date. Selecting a supervisor includes employees supervised by them during that period, while attendance metrics still cover the selected appraisal year/through date.

Disciplinary Action records actual action date, staff, action, reference and optional details. Dates cannot be future dates. Editing requires an unchanged staff identity, current revision and change reason. Administrator attribution and before/after history are saved. Only actions in the selected appraisal year through the report date are displayed. They do not change attendance/leave figures or apply an automatic score/penalty. Principal role changes and prior employment roles are not effective-dated by this feature.

All writes remain administrator-only and same-origin. Data is stored with the existing private server data, and does not go into the public source tree. Permanent employee deletion removes associated disciplinary history and supervisor links. Existing office records are not prepopulated with sample actions or assignments.
